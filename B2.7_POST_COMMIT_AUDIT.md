### Critical Observation

- `TabIcons.tsx` uses `backgroundColor: colors.wordmark` — this means the tab bar background IS the wordmark color. The **text on top of this** would need contrasting text color (likely `colors.brandText: '#FFFFFF'`).
- `MapMarkers.tsx` uses `backgroundColor: colors.wordmark` for map markers, with likely white or light text on top.
- `AuthBrandHeader.tsx` uses `color: colors.wordmark` for brand text, probably on a navy/primary background.

The contrast fix (`#E9A319` → `#C99600`) improves the wordmark-against-white ratio from ~1.9:1 to ~2.68:1, but **both fail WCAG AA 4.5:1 for normal text**. The token passes WCAG AA 3:1 for large text only.
